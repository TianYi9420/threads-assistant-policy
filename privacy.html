Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-27

"愛吃白米飯" is a personal tool used by the owner of the Threads account @baimi._627 to publish posts and manage replies through the official Threads API.

Data we access

  • Your Threads profile id and username.
  • Your own posts and the replies to them.
  • Public posts returned by keyword search, and public posts that mention you.

How we use it

Only to display this content to the account owner inside the app, and to publish posts or replies the owner has explicitly approved. AI may suggest draft text; nothing is sent without the owner's approval.

Storage and sharing

  • The access token is stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • Search results are kept for at most 24 hours.
  • We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties, and do not use it for advertising.

Revoking access

You can remove the app at any time in Threads: Settings > Account > Website permissions.

Data deletion

To request deletion of any data related to your account, open an issue using the contact link below (no personal details needed in public; we will reply to arrange it). We will delete the data within 30 days and confirm.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/TianYi9420/threads-assistant-policy/issues.
